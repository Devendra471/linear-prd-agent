Self-serve avoidability estimate

Of the 51 FO tickets, ~30–32 (60%) could plausibly be avoided with self-serve product features:

  • Real-time payment status timeline → ~14–15 Balance Pending tickets where payment is already in flight or done.
  • POD courier tracking + new-address notification → ~4–5 tickets blocked on POD physically not received.
  • TDS certificate self-upload with section auto-detect → 5–6 TDS tickets.
  • Duplicate-ticket block (same trip same category) → 5–6 duplicates.
  • Detention timer + auto-escalation → reduces frustration on 4–5 unloading tickets even if doesn't deflect.

4G. Product Recommendations

Top 3 categories to reduce tickets via product intervention

1. Real-time Payment Status Visibility in FO App (Balance Pending — 24 tickets / 47%)

  • What FO App should show: Per-trip Payment Timeline with 5 nodes — POD Received ✓ → Invoice Validated → Bank Initiated → UTR Generated (with ref no.) → Credited to Account. Show ETA per node based on historical average.
  • Proactive push: On every state transition send WhatsApp + in-app notification with the UTR reference once payment is initiated. Daily digest for trips stuck >48h in any node.
  • Self-serve flow: "Where's my payment?" CTA on each unloaded trip card → opens the timeline. If state = Credited but FO claims not received, auto-opens dispute pre-filled with UTR.
  • Estimated ticket reduction: 50–60% of Balance Pending tickets (~12–15 fewer/week, ~600+/year).

2. Detention & Trip Stuck Auto-Escalation (Trip Unloading — 5 tickets, highest frustration)

  • What FO App should show: Detention counter that starts auto on Vehicle-Reached-At-Unloading-Point + 24h. Live "Detention charges accruing: ₹X" tile.
  • Proactive push: Auto-creates internal Marketplace Trip Ops L1 escalation at 24h, L2 at 48h, L3 at 72h — without FO needing to file a ticket. Notify consignor + branch ops simultaneously.
  • Self-serve flow: "Vehicle stuck? Tap to escalate" with one-tap photo of vehicle location and complaint, geo-pinned. Eliminates the WhatsApp-call-loop seen in current resolution comments ("Trying to reach you, not picking calls").
  • Estimated ticket reduction: 60% of trip-unloading tickets (3/week), plus very large frustration / NPS impact, plus eliminates self-help precedents like FOs unloading at private warehouses.

3. TDS Self-Serve + Duplicate-Ticket Prevention (TDS & duplicate-cluster — ~11 tickets combined)

  • What FO App should show: "Your tax deduction" tile per trip with: section used (e.g. 194C), amount, downloadable TDS certificate, "Submit new section" CTA with PDF upload + auto-OCR section/PAN extraction.
  • Block at submit: When raising a ticket, run a check "You already have an open ticket for Trip X under Payment-Pending. View it instead of creating new?" — only allow new if FO confirms different issue.
  • Proactive push: At month-end, notify FOs whose TDS section is about to be auto-applied with option to submit waiver/declaration before deduction.
  • Estimated ticket reduction: 80% of TDS tickets + 100% of intra-day duplicate tickets (~8–9/week).

Ops process gaps (manual workarounds visible in CRM)

  • UTR not auto-pushed to FO at payment. Ops manually share UTR weeks after payment was made. Automate UTR-push at the moment Marketplace finance system marks payment as paid.
  • POD office-relocation not communicated. Multiple resolution comments mention "POD couriered to old office, returned (RTO)". A one-time push notification + WhatsApp blast with new office address would save 4–5 tickets/week and 2-week payment delays.
  • TDS format change broadcast via WhatsApp by individual agents. Should be a CRM-driven automated bulk message with the new format template.
  • Mandatory resolution-comment field. "Resolved" alone (seen in #4393, #4178, #4218) should be blocked by CRM. Require minimum 1 of: UTR, ETA, action-required-from-FO, escalation-target.
  • L4 trip escalations close without confirming FO outcome. Add a 24h post-close FO confirmation prompt.

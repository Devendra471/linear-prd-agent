01

Payments & Financial Automation

Remove manual money-movement work from CRM and make every trip's payment accurate, immutable and self-releasing. This theme attacks the ~78% of FO tickets that are payment-related at the root.

TaskWhat are we doing?Measurable outcomePriority
Ledger Automation in CRM
  • Overpayment settlement across FO / LSP wallets
  • Immutable trip-level payment record (Net Payable / Receivable)
 One source of truth per trip; zero ledger-mismatch disputes and manual reconciliation removed. Foundation that de-risks every other payment fix. P0
Auto-calculate TDS
  • Capture TDS details in CRM (Supplier KYC)
  • Auto-calculate TDS for new & active trips
  • Recalculate / reverse TDS on mid-month declaration change
 Accurate, audit-ready deductions with no manual computation. Targets ~80% cut in TDS tickets (~11/wk) and ends agent WhatsApp format-sharing. P0
Balance Payment Automation
  • FO can raise balance-payment status once POD is cleaned & HPOD received
  • Auto-trigger release when POD is the only blocker
 Directly targets the #1 bucket: 50–60% fewer Balance-Pending tickets (~12–15/wk) and a faster payout cycle. P0
Automated Advance Payment — Gold & Silver FOs
  • Rule-based auto-disbursal of advance to eligible Gold/Silver FOs on assignment/loading, gated by tier & exposure
 Faster advance to top FOs, fewer "advance not received" tickets, lower approval TAT and stronger retention of high-value (Gold/Silver) supply. P1
02

FO App — Transparency & Self-Serve

Give fleet owners direct visibility into their money and tax so they stop filing tickets to "see" status. Most balance tickets are already paid — the gap is visibility, not payment.

TaskWhat are we doing?Measurable outcomePriority
Trip Statement — 3-yr Financial Ledger tab
  • FO App tab showing 3 years of advance + balance + UTR + deductions, per trip
 Self-serve "where's my money" answers; deflects payment-status & statement/NDC tickets and removes ops dependency for statements. P0
FO App — TDS transparency + self-serve declaration
  • Show TDS rate at trip level + tax breakup
  • Downloadable TDS certificate
  • Self-serve declaration / section upload with OCR
 Pairs with auto-TDS to reach ~80% TDS ticket deflection; declarations become self-serve with no agent loop. P0
Ticketing for FOs not on the app
  • Omni-channel intake (WhatsApp / IVR / agent) unified into CRM with SLA tracking
 Captures the ~53% "Unknown-tier" ticket base with no lost issues; extends SLA coverage to non-app FOs. P1
03

POD Lifecycle & Collection

Digitise proof-of-delivery capture and audit. POD lag is the upstream root cause of the largest payment-ticket bucket and of multi-week payout delays.

TaskWhat are we doing?Measurable outcomePriority
Runner App
  • List all PODs due for collection
  • Upload POD directly against trips
  • POD audit via OCR service
  • Notify CT team on disputed POD for fastest resolution
 Shorter POD-to-payment cycle, fewer RTO / lost-POD delays, digital audit trail — unblocks balance payments at source. P0
04

Risk, Fraud & Partner (LSP) Controls

Protect the payout rails and cap partner exposure. These are low-volume in tickets but high-severity in financial-leakage terms.

TaskWhat are we doing?Measurable outcomePriority
Payment Fraud Check
  • Fraud checks at FO level & User level
  • Verification gate on bank-detail changes
 Prevents misrouted / fraudulent payouts and secures the bank-change flow — reduces financial leakage on the payout rail. P1
Configurable LSP Exposure Limit
  • Per-LSP configurable credit / exposure limit
  • Auto-disable new trips on breach
 Caps exposure per partner and enforces risk limits automatically — no manual monitoring of over-extension. P1
LSP App
  • Login via FT load-book app
  • View all open trips, tracking & POD status
  • Raise tickets and check status
 LSP self-serve visibility cuts ops back-and-forth and speeds POD / tracking resolution. P2
05

Demand & Fulfilment Intelligence

Help fulfilment agents work the right load first and respond faster to demand events — growth-side leverage rather than support deflection.

TaskWhat are we doing?Measurable outcomePriority
Demand Actionability Score
  • Score & rank open demand by placeability so agents prioritise placeable loads first
 Higher load-fill rate and agent productivity; less ageing of placeable loads. P1
Auto CRM Alerts
  • Automated alerts on bids received, inventory matched, and approval assigned
 Faster response to demand events, fewer missed bids, shorter fulfilment TAT. P1
Agentic AI Calling for Fulfilment Agents
  • AI voice agent to call FOs / drivers for placement and follow-ups
 Scales outreach without added headcount and speeds placement — replaces the manual call loops seen across ticket resolutions. P2
06

Compliance Automation

Generate statutory and clearance documents automatically on trigger events, removing manual document creation.

TaskWhat are we doing?Measurable outcomePriority
Automate Compliance Document Generation
  • Auto-generate compliance documents (e.g. NDC / statutory docs) on trigger events such as POD acceptance
 On-demand documents (deflects NDC-class tickets), no manual creation, audit-ready compliance output. P2
